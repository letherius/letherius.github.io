Bootstrap Carousel

This page gave me practice using a Bootstrap carousel to display several images in the same section of a website. The carousel includes indicators and controls that let the user move forward and backward through the photos.

I also made the images responsive so they adjust better depending on the size of the screen. The carousel is shorter on tablets and phones so the images don't take up most of the screen on smaller devices.
